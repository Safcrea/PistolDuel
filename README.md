# Pistol Duel

A fast, top-down 2D arena shooter where living pistols battle inside a compact sci-fi chamber. Bullets ricochet across the arena, enemy weapons can destroy one another, upgrades fall from above, and each shot is designed to feel powerful and satisfying.

> **Genre:** 2D top-down arena shooter  
> **Platform:** Mobile  
> **Engine:** Unity  
> **Status:** In development

## Game Overview

The player controls a **blue pistol** inside a futuristic combat arena. Enemy pistols are **red** and continuously enter the arena from above. Every weapon automatically aims, moves, and fires according to its current behavior and weapon type.

The objective is simple:

- Survive as long as possible.
- Destroy enemy pistols.
- Collect upgrades and power-ups.
- Build increasingly powerful weapon combinations.
- Use enemy friendly fire to create chain reactions.

The battle does not end after the arena is cleared. When all active enemies are destroyed, a new wave of enemy guns drops into the arena and combat continues.
